# WI-02 — Make each planetary takeover strategically distinct

**Goal:** Replace one-dimensional resistance with differentiated planetary encounters while retaining the satisfying incremental tempo.

## Cohesive scope

- Planet traits and factions with varying defenses, population topology, and objectives.
- Multiple infiltration approaches: covert spread, institutional capture and overt force.
- Clear response/countermeasure feedback with recovery options and no permanent deadlocks.
- Host-role or biological specialization that changes how a planet is conquered.
- Data-driven planet configurations and reproducible balance fixtures.
- UI affordances that expose new decisions without overwhelming the main screen.

## Acceptance

- At least three planet types require meaningfully different strategic choices.
- Defensive pressure can be countered through more than one viable upgrade or tactic.
- Players cannot become stuck in an unwinnable planet.
- Automated tests cover defense escalation, counterplay and multiple planet types.

**Dependency:** WI-01.
