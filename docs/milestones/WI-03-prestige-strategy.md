# WI-03 — Deepen scout prestige and enduring evolution

**Goal:** Make repeated planets exciting because the player can author increasingly different invasions.

## Cohesive scope

- Scout classes, traits, and launch payload/loadout budgeting.
- Persistent evolution branches with unlock gates, synergies and distinct build paths.
- Choice of destination or meaningful world modifiers at launch.
- New permanent capabilities beyond numerical bonuses.
- Clear lineage history and previews explaining launch choices.
- Balance rules preventing an obviously dominant loadout or irreversible underpowered build.

## Acceptance

- Two scouts with different loadouts produce observably different opening strategies.
- Multiple lineage paths are viable and open additional game controls over time.
- Launch decisions communicate what resets, what persists and what changes next.
- Save compatibility and regression tests cover the expanded prestige state.

**Dependency:** WI-01; planet variety from WI-02 informs scout balance.
